Our Story

A love that grew
quietly, and then all at once

✦

Four small chapters, one long story — told here in the order it happened.

Our Story · I

It began with a whisper of curiosity, a single glance through a small, worn hole in a kopiah. In the bustling classroom of 2010, a boy’s world paused, captivated by the new girl introducing herself. He was a mystery to her, a face in the crowd, and she was a universe he was too shy to explore. They orbited each other for the rest of primary school, two planets on separate paths, knowing so little until the day they were pulled apart.

Our Story · II

Years dissolved, but fate, it seemed, was just waiting for a better moment. Enter the online world — a digital bridge across the distance. The boy, now braver, reached out. Their screens lit up with a rekindled connection, and they became silent, constant witnesses to each other’s lives, never missing an update, cheering from afar as they grew from boy to man, from girl to woman.

Our Story · III

The road ahead was winding, but it was sunlit. To love each other meant walking a path that held its share of challenges—but also warmth, laughter, and a thousand small joys waiting to be found. They both knew it, a quiet, hopeful truth that shimmered between them. And the girl, with a heart braver than ever, entrusted him with her heart anyway. And the boy, who had grown strong but was still so weak against the force of her love, surrendered. It was the easiest, and most wonderful, choice he ever made.

Our Story · IV

Their story—once the tale of two strangers who found their way into each other’s lives—has now become a promise to walk through life together.

Faizol & Rabiatul

Before you ask

Frequently Asked

✦
Can I bring a plus-one?

Your invitation will state the number of seats reserved in your honour. If it says “and guest”, we’d love to meet them. If you’re unsure, just drop us a message — we’d rather you ask than wonder.

Are children welcome?

We’ve chosen to make this an adults-only celebration, with the exception of little ones in the bridal party. We hope this gives every parent a rare, relaxed evening off.

What should I wear?

Formal: midi or floor-length dresses, suits or smart separates, in soft warm tones — cream, sage, dusty rose, warm sand. Please avoid white and ivory.

What time should I arrive?

Aim to arrive by 3:30 PM for welcome drinks. The ceremony begins promptly at 4:00 PM, and once it starts we won’t be able to seat late guests until it concludes.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes — complimentary parking on site, with attendants to guide you. If you’d rather not drive, our shuttle leaves Kota Bharu city centre at 3:00 PM and returns after the farewell.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We’re having an unplugged ceremony — please keep phones and cameras away until we’re pronounced married. After that, photograph everything. There’s a shared album link on the tables at dinner.

What about dietary requirements?

The RSVP form has a space for allergies and dietary needs. Tell us there and our caterer will take care of the rest — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and halal options are all available.

When do I need to RSVP by?

Please reply by 1 May 2026 so we can finalise seating, meals and the shuttle. After that date we sadly can’t guarantee a seat at the table.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes. The main hall, dining area and restrooms are all step-free, with an accessible entrance at the main door. Let us know on the RSVP form if you’d like reserved seating or any other assistance.
